Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

MyMood is a private mood journal designed to work without an account or login.

Data stored on your device

MyMood stores the mood notes, reminders, photos, journal text and app settings that you choose to add. This information is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device.

App Lock

If you enable App Lock, MyMood uses the device authentication already configured on your device, such as Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. MyMood does not receive or store your biometric information.

Photos, microphone and speech recognition

MyMood only accesses photos that you explicitly select. If you use audio dictation, MyMood requests microphone and speech-recognition permission to turn your speech into text. MyMood stores the resulting text in your entry and does not save audio recordings.

Analytics and advertising

MyMood does not include advertising, third-party analytics or behavioral tracking.

Deleting your data

You can delete your diary data from Settings inside the app. Removing the app from your device may also delete locally stored data.
